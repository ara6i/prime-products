● Three buckets of speed-ups, ranked by quality cost:

  Free (no quality loss):
  1. Bigger GPU — RTX 4000 Ada (20 GB) → RTX 6000 Ada (48 GB) drops 17 s → ~11
  s. H100 (80 GB) drops to ~6 s. Same model, same weights, same output.
  2. torch.compile + xformers attention — wrap the unet at startup; ~25–30 %
  off, no pixel difference. Effectively free once compiled.
  3. Cache the preprocessing — DensePose + human parsing + OpenPose are ~3–4 s
  of the 17 s and only depend on the person photo. Cache them keyed by
  sha256(personImage) so a user's 2nd–6th try-on with different garments costs
  only the diffusion (~13 s on 4000 Ada, ~5 s on H100).
  4. Cache the IP-Adapter embedding per garment SKU — saves ~0.5 s and is what
  AIUTA exploits for the "instant on repeat" feel.

  Cheap (≤5 % quality loss):
  5. Drop steps 30 → 20 — ~11 s on 4000 Ada, ~7 s on 6000 Ada. Texture quality
  drops slightly, but at cfg=2.0 the IP-Adapter still anchors fabric detail.

  Real distillation (1 % quality loss but a week of work):
  6. LCM-LoRA on the IDM unet — finetune ~12 hrs on a small dataset, reduces 30
  steps → 4–8 steps. This is the "AIUTA secret sauce." With 8 steps you can hit
  ~3 s on RTX 6000 Ada at near-equivalent texture quality.

  - Result: same texture/silhouette, ~5× faster, ~1–2 % quality drop (usually
  invisible)
