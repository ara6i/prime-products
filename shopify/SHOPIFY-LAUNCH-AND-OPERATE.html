Launch + operate — Shopify full reference

Comprehensive companion to the submission checklist. Covers every page from Shopify's launch & built-for-shopify documentation: review process, submission flow, app statuses, listing categories, quality checks, policy violations, billing patterns, deployment, distribution, ads, marketing — plus all the items I missed in the first pass.

Top gaps to close right now

1. Confirm app.billing.tsx uses Managed App Pricing or appSubscriptionCreate. If it uses anything else (Stripe / direct cards), the app will be auto-rejected. 30-min audit.

2. PCD L1.6 — opt-out for automated decisions. Our size recommendation IS automated decision-making. Need a "request manual review" pathway OR a privacy-policy clause + UI consent step on the try-on widget.

3. Apply for Protected Customer Data BEFORE submitting. Once the app is in review, you cannot apply. Submit the PCD form from the App Store review page first.

4. Email allowlist. Add app-submissions@shopify.com, app-audits@shopify.com, and noreply@shopify.com to allowed senders for the inbox the Partner email points to. Missed reviewer emails = automatic suspension risk.

01 About the Shopify App Store

Where merchants discover apps. Your listing is the foundation for every discovery surface — App Store browse, in-admin recommendations, Sidekick suggestions all draw from your public listing's description, features, pricing, and reviews.

The four pillars of being on the App Store

  1. Get approved — meet App Store requirements + pass review
  2. Charge correctly — use Shopify Billing API (Managed Pricing or programmatic)
  3. Market + support — listing visibility, marketing, customer support
  4. Improve quality — earn Built for Shopify status for promotion + visibility boosts

Revenue share quick facts

  • Eligible developers pay 0% revenue share on the first $1,000,000 USD earned
  • Above that: 15% revenue share (reduced from 20% via the reduced-rev-share program)
  • Apply for the reduced rate in Partner Dashboard if you qualify

What surfaces your listing appears on

  • Shopify App Store (apps.shopify.com)
  • In-admin app recommendations
  • Sidekick suggestions
  • Category pages
  • Search results
  • Third-party search engines (Google) — fully visible apps only

02 The app review process — statuses + flow

Your app moves through 4 statuses during review. Knowing the flow helps you predict what to expect at each step.

Status flow

StatusWhat it meansWhat you do
DraftYou're still building the listing. Fix issues on the App Store review page before you can submit.Click Submit your app when ready
SubmittedApp is in the review queue. Confirmation email arrives.Wait. Withdraw at any time via the status banner.
PausedApp didn't meet core requirements; reviewer can't proceed. Email outlines required changes.Fix → click Submit fixes
ReviewedReviewer assigned + needs to discuss specific items. Email outlines next steps.Reply to continue the conversation
PublishedApp is live on the App Store.Begin marketing + monitor reviews

Approximate timing

  • First response (Submitted → Paused or Reviewed): typically 1–2 weeks
  • Each round of feedback: 3–7 days reviewer turnaround
  • Total time to Published: 2–6 weeks if you respond quickly and don't pile up issues
Tip: Reviewers handle multiple apps in parallel. Reply quickly + thoroughly — letting threads sit adds days and risks suspension.

04 Temporary suspensions

Shopify can temporarily suspend a Partner from submitting an app. Suspensions affect only the app currently in review — not other apps, not the Partner account.

What triggers a suspension

  • Consecutively failing to address issues highlighted by the reviewer after two or more exchanges
  • Repeatedly submitting the app with new and growing numbers of issues
  • Failing to respond to review emails after repeated attempts
  • Refusing to accept the outcome of exemption requests

What happens during suspension

  • App moves to Suspended status
  • Status banner shows the date you can resubmit on
  • Other apps owned by the Partner are unaffected
  • The Partner account itself is unaffected
  • Other people in the Partner organization are unaffected
Repeat suspensions stack. Each subsequent suspension is longer than the last. Treat each reviewer email like a same-day SLA.

05 Pass app review — pre-flight tests

Final tests + admin tasks to do before clicking Submit. Shopify only reviews production-ready apps.

Pre-flight checklist

  1. Run the AI self-review in your agent chat: /shopify-app-store-review
  2. Install your app on a development store — exercise install flow, OAuth grant, every feature
  3. Check OAuth installs for errors — App URL + redirect URLs must redirect merchants to OAuth grant. With Shopify managed installation, you can skip this.
  4. Test billing system — create a test charge with "test": true. Switch to "test": false before going live or merchants won't be charged.
  5. Apply for Protected Customer Data access if you touch any protected fields — must be done BEFORE submit
  6. Test reinstall — install → uninstall → reinstall must trigger OAuth grant again
  7. Test in incognito with 3rd-party cookies blocked — full flow must still work via session tokens

Common review problems (and how to avoid each)

EmbeddingSwitches between embedded + non-embedded versionsUse App Bridge consistently · session token authenticationYes

06 App listing categories

Categorization drives discovery. Primary tag required; secondary optional. Shopify's review team verifies your categorization is accurate.

The seven top-level categories

  1. Sales channels — sell to customers via channels
  2. Finding products — source / find products for the store
  3. Selling products — payment options, pricing, digital goods, custom products
  4. Orders and shipping — order management, fulfillment, inventory, returns
  5. Store design — theme customization, page builders, images / media, design elements, notifications, content, internationalization
  6. Marketing and conversion — marketing automation, ads, email, SMS, conversion optimization
  7. Store management — accounting, finance, analytics, productivity

Category recommendation for our app

PrimeStyle Try-On fits into Store design → Images and media → 3D/AR/VR ("Apps that help customers visualize and try on products in real life"). Optional secondary tag in Marketing and conversion → Conversion optimization if we lean into the conversion-rate angle.

Category structured features

Each category lets you select up to 25 structured feature tags per category field. They appear on the listing and help merchants compare apps. Update anytime — no appeal needed.

Changing categorization later

If your app's capabilities change, submit an appeal via the link in the app submission form. Shopify reviews and replies with approve/reject.

Tip: Research apps similar to yours on the App Store and use the same categories — Shopify's review team has already validated them.

07 App quality checks (ongoing)

Separate from initial review and BFS annual reviews. Quality checks are ongoing audits Shopify runs to make sure your app still meets current requirements — which can be updated without notice.

How it works

  1. Shopify selects your app for a quality check
  2. You receive an email from app-audits@shopify.com with required changes
  3. Respond within 7 days — failure to respond demotes your app
  4. Make required changes within 30 days — failure can result in your app being unpublished
  5. After making changes, reply to the quality check email describing what you did
  6. App Excellence Team reviews; if changes meet current requirements, the quality check is complete
Hard deadlines: 7-day response, 30-day fix. Don't ignore these — quality check failures lead to demotion or unpublishing.

What you should do every quarter

  • Re-read App Store requirements — they change without notice
  • Re-grep code for new deprecation patterns (REST .json, App Bridge versions, etc.)
  • Re-run Lighthouse on your storefront block in a fresh Dawn install
  • Confirm GDPR webhook handlers still respond correctly

08 Policy violations

Violating the Partner Program Agreement or API License & Terms of Use triggers enforcement actions. Severity depends on the policy.

The two binding agreements

  • Partner Program Agreement — shopify.com/partners/terms
  • API License and Terms of Use — shopify.com/legal/api-terms

Common violation categories

  • Misuse of "Shopify" branding (in app name, URL, icon, etc.)
  • Bypassing Shopify checkout
  • Bypassing Shopify Billing API
  • Fake reviews or testimonials
  • Misleading claims in listing
  • Storing Shopify credentials insecurely
  • Calling APIs outside approved scopes
  • Using merchant data for unauthorized purposes

Enforcement actions (escalating)

  1. Warning email + required fix
  2. App suspension
  3. App removal from App Store
  4. Partner account restriction or removal

Reporting violations by other apps

You can report a competitor's policy violation at shopify.com/legal/tools/report-an-issue/report-a-partner-violation.

10 Regain lost BFS status

BFS can be lost between reviews if criteria slip. Auto-loss is recoverable on its own — no resubmission needed.

Automated criteria (lose after 60 days of failure)

  • Uninstalls cleanly (theme app extensions properly remove)
  • Embedded in Shopify admin
  • Minimizes impact on checkout speed
  • Admin performance — meets 75th-percentile Web Vitals targets
  • Minimum number of installs
  • Minimum number of reviews
  • Minimum app rating

Track these in real time on Partner Dashboard → Apps → Distribution.

Manual criteria (ongoing)

Apps may be reviewed at any time for design, integration, or other manual items. 60 days to fix anything found.

Auto-regain

BFS is restored automatically when all criteria pass again. No reapplication needed.

13 Support your customers

All public apps must offer at least one support channel. Shopify doesn't do merchant support for third-party apps — that's on you.

Required: support email address

Always required, even if email isn't your preferred channel. Set in Partner Dashboard → app listing → Support section.

Optional support channels

  • Support portal URL — forum, ticketing system, or knowledge base
  • Phone number — display-only; no real-time call routing

Where merchants see "Get support" links

  • App settings page in Shopify admin
  • Top-right action menu of your app's home page in Shopify admin
  • App Store listing

Localized support

You can override the support channel per-locale. Useful if you have native-speaking support in different regions. Set per-locale in Partner Dashboard → localized listing → Support section.

Custom support events (advanced)

Apps in App Home can extend the "Get support" action via App Bridge — for example, opening a real-time chat directly in your app instead of a contact form.

# Generate a support_link extension
shopify app generate extension --template support_link --name support-link

Then update the extension's TOML to point to a route in your app:

[[extensions.targeting]]
target = "admin.app.support.link"
text = "t:text"
url = "app://help"  # opens /help in the embedded admin

Test with shopify app dev and ship with shopify app deploy.

Recommended response targets

  • First response: within 24 hours (business days)
  • Resolution: target ≤ 5 business days
  • Sub-95% on-time response: BFS criteria flag

14 Billing & Managed Pricing

Apps on the App Store must use Shopify's billing API. No Stripe. No direct credit cards.

Two paths

ApproachUse when
Managed App PricingStandard subscription plans (free/pro/enterprise tiers, annual options, free trials). Shopify hosts the plan-selection page; you configure plans in Partner Dashboard. Recommended for most apps.
Programmatic billing via appSubscriptionCreateNeed usage-based pricing not supported by Managed, custom proration, complex plan combinations, per-store custom pricing.

Managed Pricing setup

  1. Partner Dashboard → App → Distribution → Pricing → enable Managed Pricing
  2. Define plans: name, price, interval, free trial length, included features
  3. Generate the plan-selection welcome link
  4. In your app, redirect un-subscribed merchants to the welcome link
  5. Subscribe to billing webhooks: app_subscriptions/update, app/uninstalled

Public vs private plans

TypeUse case
PublicVisible to all merchants — most plans
PrivateHidden from catalog; only via direct link. Use for custom/enterprise deals or beta-tester pricing.

Welcome links + plan selection page

Direct links to the plan-selection page or a specific plan. Useful in onboarding emails, marketing pages, or in-app upgrade buttons. Generated per-plan or as a shared "all plans" link.

Proration logic

  • Upgrades — charged immediately, prorated for remainder of cycle
  • Downgrades — take effect next cycle
  • Shopify handles proration math automatically

Free trials + free testing

  • Free trial duration — set per plan (typical: 7 / 14 / 30 days). Trial begins on plan acceptance.
  • Free testing — dev stores get test charges automatically. Switch to "test": false in code before production.

Test charges

On dev stores, charges are always test charges — no real money. Programmatic billing accepts "test": true on charge requests; double-check it's false before launch.

Webhook topics

  • app_subscriptions/update — fires on plan change, cancel, freeze, unfreeze
  • app/uninstalled — fires on uninstall
  • app_purchases_one_time/update — for one-time charges

Supported currencies

USD by default. Other currencies via the GraphQL Billing API — but rev share + payouts convert to USD at standard rates.

Programmatic billing — required mutations

  • appSubscriptionCreate — start a subscription
  • appSubscriptionCancel — cancel
  • appUsageRecordCreate — log usage for usage-based plans
  • appSubscriptionLineItemUpdate — change a plan's max charge
  • appPurchaseOneTimeCreate — one-time fee
  • appCreditCreate — issue a credit

Billing best practices

  • Show pricing clearly in-app + in the listing
  • Allow plan changes in-app (BFS req)
  • Notify merchants 7+ days before usage caps are hit
  • On plan downgrade, gracefully disable premium features (visually grey)
  • On uninstall, cancel subscription via webhook within minutes
  • Test all paths with `"test": true` before shipping

15 Subscription patterns

Time-based

Flat fee per cycle. Examples: $29/month, $290/year.

Usage-based

Per-event pricing. Each charge is capped by cappedAmount; once hit, no more charges that cycle.

Example: $0.05 per try-on, capped at $50/month.

appUsageRecordCreate(
  description: "Try-on rendered",
  price: { amount: 0.05, currencyCode: USD },
  subscriptionLineItemId: "..."
)

Combined time + usage

Base fee + usage. ONE subscription with TWO line items.

Example: $99/mo base + $0.02 per try-on after 1000.

Complex / tiered pricing

Each tier = a separate plan. Migrate merchants between plans on usage thresholds: appSubscriptionCancel → new appSubscriptionCreate.

Update max charge

Approaching cappedAmount? Use appSubscriptionLineItemUpdate to request a new approval. Merchant gets a Shopify-hosted approval page.

Subscription types — under the hood

  • RecurringApplicationCharge — standard subscription
  • UsageCharge — per-event pricing on top of a recurring subscription
  • ApplicationCharge — one-time fee
  • ApplicationCredit — credit (negative charge)

Deferral

Subscription charges can be deferred so the merchant approves now but isn't billed until later. Useful for "first month free" promotions.

Cancelling subscriptions

  • Merchant-initiated: uninstall app, decline charge, manually cancel
  • App-initiated: appSubscriptionCancel mutation
  • Shopify-initiated: if merchant's Shopify subscription becomes inactive (frozen)

16 Subscription discounts

Offer percentage or fixed-price discounts on subscriptions to attract new merchants or retain existing ones. Use the GraphQL Admin API.

How it works

  • Apply for a set number of billing cycles (e.g. 20% off for 6 cycles) OR indefinitely
  • Works on annual + 30-day subscriptions
  • Available for new and existing subscribers
  • Taxes, Shopify service fees, and revenue share are calculated on the discounted price

When discounts begin

  • Apply on the next billing cycle after creation
  • Add discount during a trial → trial ends immediately and discount applies right away
  • Set number of cycles starts counting from the moment the discount applies (trial periods ignored)
  • No cycle limit = limitless discount

Proration interaction

  • If the new subscription requires proration: the discounted price is used to calculate the prorated amount
  • If no proration is required: discount starts at next renewal

Removing discounts

  • Create a new subscription without the discount; merchant approves
  • Until merchant approves, they're charged the discounted price for the rest of the current interval
Use cases for us: "First 3 months 50% off" for new installs, holiday promo discounts, win-back discounts for uninstalled merchants who reinstall.

17 App credits, one-time charges & refunds

One-time purchases

Use appPurchaseOneTimeCreate for non-recurring items (e.g. add-on pack, setup fee).

App credits

Use appCreditCreate to issue a credit to a merchant's next invoice. Common cases:

  • Refund part of a charge (Shopify doesn't process direct refunds — credits are the mechanism)
  • Promotional credit ("$10 off your next month")
  • SLA / outage compensation

Limits: credit cannot exceed the amount the merchant paid in the last 30 days. Credits reduce your earnings.

Refund app charges

True refunds (money back to card) require Shopify support — not API. For most cases, App Credits are the equivalent.

View charges & earnings

  • Partner Dashboard → Apps → app → Overview / Insights / App history (visual + CSV export)
  • Partner GraphQL API — programmatic access to transactions, shopifyPaymentsBalance, app(id) usage.

Shopify provides a step-by-step guide for deploying RR7 / Remix apps to Cloud Run:

  1. Containerize the app (Dockerfile)
  2. Push to Google Container Registry
  3. Deploy to Cloud Run with environment variables for Shopify credentials
  4. Set up Cloud SQL or Atlas for session storage

App versions

Shopify tracks every deploy as a version. Key commands:

shopify app deploy           # Push current code as a new version
shopify app versions list    # List deployed versions
shopify app versions release # Activate a specific version

Versions allow staged rollout — push a new version but don't release; test on a single store; release to everyone when confident.

Deploy + release flow

  1. shopify app deploy — creates a new version (not yet active)
  2. Test in dev store
  3. shopify app versions release --version=... — activate for all merchants

CD pipeline shape (multi-piece app)

  1. Push to main → CI runs tests
  2. If green: build admin → push to droplet, restart PM2
  3. Build theme extension → shopify app deploy (creates new version)
  4. Activate the new version automatically OR via manual approval (recommended for theme extension changes)
  5. Backend deploys independently to droplet

20 Sunsetting an app

If we ever wind down, Shopify has a documented graceful path.

  1. Notify all installed merchants ≥ 30 days in advance via in-app banner + email
  2. Give them an export path for any data we hold
  3. Stop new installs (set to limited visibility)
  4. Honor billing cancellations promptly
  5. On D-day: stop accepting webhooks; reply 410 Gone to API hits
  6. Run the shop/redact handler on every shop after the 48h Shopify grace
  7. Remove the listing from Partner Dashboard

23 Revenue share

Shopify's cut depends on per-app revenue tier.

  • 0% revenue share on the first $1,000,000 USD earned per partner — eligible developers only
  • Above $1M: 15% revenue share (reduced from 20% via reduced-rev-share program)
  • Built for Shopify status doesn't change rev share — it's about visibility + promotion

Apply for the reduced rate in Partner Dashboard if you qualify. Most new apps qualify.

24 App Store ads — overview

Paid placement above organic App Store search results. Bidding model. Run after launch + first reviews are in.

How it works

  1. You bid on keywords merchants might search ("size chart", "virtual try on", "fashion fit")
  2. Your ad shows above organic results when a merchant searches
  3. You pay per click (CPC), not per impression
  4. Shopify takes a small fee on top of the CPC

Setup checklist

  1. App is fully visible (not limited)
  2. App listing complete + reviewed by Shopify
  3. Set ad permissions (who in your team can manage)
  4. Add a payment method for ad billing
  5. Create your first ad campaign with target keywords + max bid
  6. Track performance via the ads dashboard

Ad billing

Charged separately from app earnings. Invoice from Shopify monthly. Credit card is the only supported payment method.

Ad permissions

Roles: ad admins (create / pause / delete) and ad viewers (read-only). Set in Partner Dashboard → team settings.

Search ads vs homepage ads

  • Search ads — appear above organic search results when merchants search for your keywords
  • Homepage ads — appear on the App Store homepage; broader reach, less targeted

Recommended for us

  • Wait until: ≥ 5 reviews, BFS submitted, at least one strong testimonial in listing copy
  • First campaign: ~$200–$500 budget, narrow keywords
  • Iterate weekly based on CTR + install conversion

25 Manage ads + check performance

Ad statuses

  • Active — being shown for searches (assuming a winning bid). Charged per click.
  • Paused — campaign exists but not shown
  • Ended — campaign has finished
  • Pending — under review or not yet started

What you can edit while running

  • Ad name, daily budget
  • Device targeting (desktop, mobile)
  • Geotargeting
  • Search keywords (add / remove / change bid)
  • CPC bids per keyword

What you CANNOT edit (must stop + create new campaign)

  • The app itself
  • Ad placement type (search vs homepage)
  • Plan-based merchant targeting

Update keywords flow

Partner Dashboard → Apps → App ads → click campaign:

  • Add keywords: click + Keywords, enter terms + initial bid
  • Update bid: edit the amount in the "Bid per click" column → Save
  • Pause/unpause keyword: use the toggle in the Status column
  • Remove keywords: select rows → Actions → Remove keywords

26 Ads FAQ

Why isn't my ad appearing in search results?

  • Bid for a keyword isn't high enough
  • App isn't relevant for the keywords you're bidding on
  • Ad budget exhausted for the day
  • Search was made via VPN
  • Unusual traffic patterns (load shedding)

If two of my ads share keywords, which one wins?

They enter the auction together; one is chosen based on bid + relevance.

Can I run ads in non-English languages?

Yes — bid on non-English keywords. Accents and special characters are stripped from impressions.

Do ads show to merchants who already installed my app?

No. But uninstalled merchants are eligible to see ads again.

Do ads show on shops not compatible with my app?

No — Shopify respects the install requirements you specified in your listing.

Minimum bid?

Yes. The system enforces a minimum bid; sub-minimum bids don't display.

Minimum daily budget?

$5.00 per day.

Can I run multiple ads for the same app?

Yes.

Can ads link to a page other than my listing?

No. All ads link to the App Store listing for consistent merchant experience.

How is ad credit applied?

Automatically applied to all currently-running search ads. Credit card is only charged after credit is exhausted.

Other payment methods?

Credit card only.
